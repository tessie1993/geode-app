## Build and ABI

- The root `CMakeLists.txt` builds the native core, plus `core/CMakeLists.txt` for the `geode_core` static library.
- `app/src/main/cpp/*_jni.cpp` only marshal values into the `extern "C"` ABI in `core/api/geode_api.h`.
- projectM gets `tools/projectm-v4.1.7-render-fbo-backport.patch` at configure time.
- projectM also gets `tools/projectm-v4.1.7-shader-fallback.patch`, which reports the shaders it swapped for the default (`projectm_get_dropped_shaders`).
- The submodules are `third_party/{projectm,taglib,cgltf,meshoptimizer,basis_universal,kissfft,libdatachannel,mbedtls}`.
- libdatachannel and its Mbed TLS (`core/cast/mbedtls_user_config.h`) build only for `core/cast/WebRtcLink`, Show on a computer's WebRTC; `core/cast/` is the only native directory that may include them or a socket header (`tools/network-gate.sh`).
- `core/cast/LiveServer` is the cast's HTTP server on plain POSIX sockets, bound to the phone's LAN address; `LiveHttp` (requests and answers) and `LiveSegments` (the segment window and playlist) hold its decisions and include no socket header.
- KISS FFT's header-only `kissfft.hh` is the transform under `core/sound/Fft`.
- cgltf is compiled in `GltfImport.cpp`.
- meshoptimizer's simplifier and codecs build as `geode_meshopt`, in `core/CMakeLists.txt`.
- Basis Universal's encoder, transcoder and zstd build as `geode_basisu`, in `core/CMakeLists.txt`.

## `core/sound`: hearing

- `Silence` is the wallpaper's and dream's rest-when-silent rule, polled through `geode_silence_*`.
- The sound engine is MilkDrop's FFT path beside a TouchDesigner-style per-sample filter bank, stepped once per rendered frame into a `GeodeSoundFrame`.
- How much the music is comes from absolute loudness, never from the song's own average.
- `LufsMeter` (fosfora's) gives BS.1770 momentary and short-term LUFS (`loudnessM`/`loudnessS`) and the one silence gate every detector reads.
- `busy` is onset and drum density, the beat.
- `Transient` (`core/sound/Transient`) hears the sharp start of any sound per sample: a fast follower over a slow one in the bank's low, middle and high bands and the whole mix, past a threshold read from the last second (median plus MAD), no tempo.
- Its frame fields are `transient` (a trigger), `transientEnv` and `transientSharpness`, gated and weighted like a hit.
- The reaction sources `Transient`, `TransientEnv` and `TransientSharpness` read those fields.
- The LFO source Transient (`ModSource::Transient`) follows `transientEnv`; it followed the flux onset's `onsetEnv` before.
- `drive` = loudness × texture.
- `sound/DriveGain` turns `drive` into the gain that `MotionField`, `CameraRig`, `NoteResponse` and `Reactions` multiply the magnitude of audio-driven motion by.
- The gain is full at a drive of 0.5 (`kDriveFull`), an ordinary well-mastered song, since a hit is weighed by the drive in the engine too.
- The frame's `flux` compares each bin with its neighbourhood ~30 ms before (`sound/OnsetFlux`, a maximum filter a semitone wide), so vibrato and slides do not read as onsets.
- The relative bass/mid/treble ratios only shape the pulse.
- Settings › Audio's input trim is the analysis-only gain for a mic or another app's audio.
- A live renderer hears the player only on a ring a sink plays (`sound::player::heardAsPlayer`, set by `geode_viz_feed_ring`); the wallpaper's output mix hears no player.

## `core/sound`: the musical layer

- `Rhythm`: tempo, beat, bar, phrase, on a fixed 10 ms hop of the written samples.
- `Harmony`: chroma, key, chord, tension, scale from `ScaleTable.inc`.
- `ScaleTable.inc` is generated by `tools/musical_intelligence.py` with `docs/musical-intelligence/`.
- `Form`: novelty, sections, build-up, drop, section state.
- `Emotion` reads the frame as a feeling: valence, energy, tension and GEMS-9, from `EmotionTable.inc`.
- No tempo enters the feeling: its energy and valence come from the sound alone.
- `EmotionTable.inc` holds a mode vector per scale and a key character per key, generated by `tools/music_emotion.py`.

## `core/viz`: renderer, scenes, catalogue

- `core/viz` holds the renderer, the scene registry, `scenes/`, `fluid/`, `liquid/`, `physics/` and `compute/`.
- `Catalogue` is the style catalogue: every style id, kind, family, param scope and control range.
- Kotlin asks the catalogue one question at a time (`geode_catalogue_style_ids`, `_picker_names`, `_picker_ids`, `_resolve`, `_preset_style`, `_scope_of`, `_reaches`, `_override`).
- `catalogue::text()` is the same catalogue as one table, for the fixture and the tools; it does not cross JNI.
- Customize › Motion's Spin (field `motionDrift`, the old Spin drift's wire name) scales the turn of the styles `catalogue::spinRate` lists, shows only on them (scope `SPIN`, per style), holds them still at 0 and is never rolled.
- The catalogue's presets section, `CataloguePresets`, is the bundled looks by param name; `data/BuiltInPresets` reads it through `geode_catalogue_presets_text`.
- `ShapeGraph` (`core/viz/ShapeGraph`) is the morphing shape graph: up to 8 primitives that union, blend, cut and mask in a one-level CSG list, packed as 8 × 4 vec4.
- Its target is a pure function of the play seed, Spawn, Morph, Formula, Fractal and Shapes; numbers glide, primitive and operation changes crossfade by weight, shapes grow from and shrink to 0, and heads drift (translation only) on a Speed-scaled clock.
- `ShapeGraph::sample` mirrors lib_shapes' distance and colour maths for the host tests (`core/tests/shape_graph_test`).
- The renderer ticks one `ShapeGraph` a frame in `resolveParams` (`Renderer::stepShapeGraph`, the frame's play seed), and `ShaderScene::uploadShapes` hands it as `uShapes[32]`, `uShapeCount`, `uShapeFold`, `uShapeMelt` to a program that reads them (`SceneHost::shapeGraph`).
- `lib_shapes` draws the graph (`shapesScene`, `sminCol`) and melts it into a style's field by `uShapeMix` (`shapesInto`, identity at 0); `shapesHue` gives the hit its palette index.
- The Shapes param (`shapes`, wire slot 198, LfoTarget 79) is uploaded as `uShapeMix` and scoped to `Scope::Shapes` (`kShapeStyles`: `chrome_nebula`, `liss`, `solar`).
- `Palette` (`core/viz/Palette`) is Customize › Look's palette stack; `Renderer::uploadPaletteRamp` bakes it into one 256-texel ramp that every shader style reads through `pal()` (`uPalLut`).
- A shader style with no stack set draws the look's own palette (`palette`, its hue overrides or your colours' stops).
- Retired with the stack in place: Palette 2 and Palette blend (`palette2`, `paletteMix`, their overrides), Colour map (`paletteLut`) and Color cycle (`colorCycle`, `cycleSpeed`); an old look naming them loads and draws its own palette.
- A stack holds up to 8 palettes of up to 16 colours, each Smooth or Steps, Mirror or Loop, RGB, hue or OKLab, a place per colour and one colour's Shades, Flat or To black; Share (Soft or Hard edges) or Mix by amount, full range off.
- Its library is `PaletteTable.inc` (old `palette` ramps 0..20, colour maps 21..25, then newer, Neon glow at 34); it also reads an old look's colours as a stack and keeps the picker's recent colours; text in, text out, `geode_palette_*`.
- Classic rows 12..20 are retired (`SceneParams::kListedPalettes`, `palette::offered`): kept so a saved index draws, offered by no picker or roll.
- How much each palette is used and where along the stack the colours sit are scene params (`paletteAmount0..7`, `paletteShift`, read by `palette::amountsOf`), so waves, envelopes, routes and the macro bank reach them; LFO targets `PALETTE_AMOUNT_1`, `_3`..`_8` and `PALETTE_SHIFT` (luminance targets: 3 Hz, the route floors). Palette 2's amount is `PALETTE_MIX`, the old Palette blend's ordinal.
- `fluid/` is the stable-fluids solver: MacCormack advection, batched splats, a multigrid pressure solve, heat and buoyancy.
- The fluid solver's pure maths is `FluidMath`.
- `FlowField` is the velocity field Liquid mode reads, kicked by touch and drops.
- The touch smear (`core/viz/TouchSmear`, Settings › Touch › Smear): touch play's fingers kick the shared `FlowField` and the composite bends the finished picture by it (`uFlow`).
- The fingers' maths (`core/viz/TouchGesture`): touch play's points in NDC, the two-finger pan, zoom, twist and pinch step, and touch play's stir; `VisualGestures.kt` keeps the pointer loop and which gesture fired.
- The smear runs with Liquid off, live only, as an optional pass (`geode_viz_set_touch_smear`).
- `liquid/LiquidMath` is Liquid mode's pure maths: thermal modes, inject weight, the random endless dive, gestures, born clock.
- `liquid/FeedbackMotion` is MilkDrop's per-frame UV transform (zoom, zoomexp, stretch, warp, rotation about a centre, translation) made per second, the sound's push on it, the mirror fold, and what the renderer asks of it (`TrailAsk`, `fromDive`).
- `LiquidPass` is a full-resolution feedback ping-pong between the scene target and the composite.
- `LiquidPass` samples the last frame through that transform (`lib_feedback`, the same maths in GLSL), then the `FlowField` velocity, folds it back at the edges, and re-pours the style through `liquid_frag`.
- `core/viz/CustomizeMap`'s text (version 2) carries each category's tab title and each section's header (`title`, `sectionTitle`).
- `core/viz/CustomizeRows` is what each Customize control draws as (`geode_customize_rows`): widget, field, words, value, range, default, unit and steps.
- `CustomizeRows` also holds when a control hides: Catch pull and radius without catch points, Liquid's sliders with Liquid off, Dive speed with neither the endless zoom nor Liquid, Fluid's glow and sunray sliders with those off.
- `CustomizeRows` hides the whole pages (Your colours, waves, envelopes, shader code, the artwork button) while the panel's search holds text, and matches the rest by key or words.
- A Cymatics style that holds its plate shows a tip naming it (`catalogue::overrideOf`) where the Geometry pick would be.
- `core/viz/PanelText` holds the panel's words by their old resource id (`geode_panel_text`); `panel::format` fills `%1$s` placeholders.
- `core/viz/StyleNames` holds the style and family display names (`geode_style_label`, `geode_style_family_label`); a style with none reads as its id in words.
- `core/viz/PalettePanel` is Your colours' stop editor as text (`geode_palette_maker_*`), the stack card's rows (`geode_palette_stack_card`) and the palette list (`geode_palette_list_view`).
- An old hue gradient opens in Your colours as 4 stops (`kMakerHueStops`) and goes on the stack as 5 (`kStackHueStops`).
- Expert › Shader code shows on Fluid only (`core/viz/CustomizeMap`, `Show::ShaderCode`), with the custom force and dye editors; a shader style has no box for its own fragment shader any more.
- A look's saved custom shader still applies (`core/viz/ShaderOverrides`, `geode_shader_override_*`).
- Your colours are native (`core/viz/Colour.hpp`, `geode_colour_*`, `geode_palette_*`): up to five stops, scene params `paletteStop0..4` (packed 0xRRGGBB), that loop seamlessly; shaders read them through `lib_palette`'s `palStops`.
- The shell's accent seed from a palette (the middle of its hue span at fixed HSL 0.85 / 0.6) is `colour::paletteAccent`, `geode_colour_palette_accent`; `VisualAccent` (contrast lift, Material roles) is still Kotlin.
- `core/viz/ParamsJson` (`geode_params_write_json`, `_read_json`) writes and reads the scene params object of presets, links, template looks and the saved look, in the text org.json wrote.
- `core/viz/ParamsPalette` is the look's own palette in its params: whether it draws one, clearing it, your colours, a hue gradient (`geode_params_uses_custom_palette`, `_palette_stops`, `_clear_custom_palette`, `_apply_stops`, `_apply_gradient`).
- `core/viz/LookFiles` holds the user's look files, text in and text out: the file stem rule (`geode_file_safe_name`, `_safe_chars`) and list order (`geode_looks_order`).
- `LookFiles` reads and writes saved palettes (`geode_saved_palette_*`): the id rule, defaults and clamps, the old-id migration, applying one to a look.
- `LookFiles` reads and writes presets (`geode_preset_read`, `_write`, `_milk_file_name`): the envelope beside the params object, its slider ranges and the number guard, retired styles resolved.
- `LookFiles` names the template library's files, makes an import's name unique and holds the bundled starter templates (`geode_template_*`).
- `core/viz/RandomizeAll` (`geode_randomize_all_*`) is the two-circle gesture's Randomize everything.
- Customize › Layers is `core/viz/LayerState`: on, source, picked style, knob, blend and mix, process-wide; an export pins it.
- Native keeps the layer: `geode_layer_edit` changes one field of it and returns the line the shell saves; `geode_layer_set` takes the saved line back at start.
- A layer's source is the user's pick (USER), Auto Director's pick at its style moments (DIRECTOR) or a knob's turn (MACRO), the owner's three choices (2026-11-12).
- `core/viz/LayerPick` turns the source into a style per renderer each frame; with nothing picked it suits the active style (same look group, another family, by a hash of the active id), never the active style while another exists.
- Auto Director's layer draws from Auto's pool (`Trait::AutoDraw`) on the play's seed; a knob walks the same pool in catalogue order with a dead band.
- `geode_layer_values`, `_list` and `_shown` are everything Customize › Layers draws and offers (`layer_pick::editor`); the shell keeps no list and parses no text.
- The layer draws into its own target (`fboL_`), so it runs through a style transition and keeps its last ready style while the next builds.
- The composite lays the layer over the transition's result, the active style the base: mix 0 is the active style alone.
- The layer's mix is the page's plus the `layerMix` param, which target `LAYER_MIX` (a luminance target) writes, so waves, envelopes, routes and knobs move it.
- `core/viz/Shake` and `core/viz/TapAlong` decide the easter eggs: a firm shake, four taps on the heard beat.

## Native stage

- `core/stage` is the native stage: one render thread, EGL context and window surface per surface, and one process-wide vsync thread; its design is [native-stage.md](native-stage.md).
- The stage's C ABI is `core/api/geode_stage_api.h`; no JNI calls it yet.

## Per-frame chain

The renderer runs these in order each frame.

- `Director`, the auto-director: at sections and drops the feeling decides a behaviour look, held until the next.
- The director's five axes are Energy, Speed, Complexity, Space and Chaos: offsets on scene params and the camera's music moves; never the user's macro bank.
- The director also sets the camera's softness and the breath dial by a factor; a dial at 0 stays off; it never touches Spin (`motionDrift`).
- The director is base plus modulation, MilkDrop's rule.
- The director's axis push scales into the room a slider has left above its default, so a slider between its default and the end still moves the picture (`director::applyAxes`).
- Owner's rule: the director never touches a colour param and plays no route of its own, and nothing in it follows the frame.
- The director remembers the look it first chose for each section Form names, the way it was heading and the style it was first shown on.
- A section is decided when Form names it (one downbeat after the event), so the look and the style change together, knowing whether it is back.
- Looks land on a downbeat when a beat is heard, over 2 bars for calm feelings down to a beat for driven ones (`Director::changeBars`).
- While Form's state is a build the director's axes lean toward the drop over four bars; the drop takes the lean away.
- A returning section morphs into its look grown on, further the way it was heading with each return, seeded (`Director::grow`); never a copy, never a cut.
- A drop cuts at once; one that opens a section cuts to the grown look of the section the last drop opened, and settles when Form names it.
- The song's arc moves Complexity, Detail, Fractal and Formula toward the peak with Form's section state and the phrases heard (`director::arcLevel`, `applyArc`).
- `director::applyArc` is the one seam that names the arc's params; a name the param table lacks is skipped.
- Every director number is an Auto Director setting in `core/viz/DirectorTuning` (key, label, help, range, default), pinned with an export in `EngineSettings`.
- At a style moment the director names the next style (`StyleIntent`); `core/viz/DirectorStyle` picks it from Auto's pool: a return's family stepped on, else the next along the catalogue.
- The renderer whose moments the shell takes publishes that intent (`Renderer::takeDirectorStyleMoments`); `geode_auto_pick_among` reads it once.
- `DirectorReach` is the director's reach past the look when Visuals › Settings asks.
- Before the look, the reach shuffles, and the Customize randomizer rolls some categories at a section, drop or long stretch from the play seed; sliders glide and toggles flip on the moment.
- After its axes, the reach adds three slow waves and three sound followers of its own.
- All reach offsets sit over the user's params and keep off kept controls, the style's scope, a control the user just moved and the hand mask.
- `NoteResponse` (Note & key reactions): notes, chords, key and mood read into the six style knobs only, drums weighted by measured percussiveness.
- `NoteResponse` writes no scene param and no colour, so what moves on a note, chord or a drop is the reaction matrix alone.
- `NoteResponse`'s PULSE follows the beat and bar phases while the beat is sure (beatConf 0.2 to 0.45 blends them in) and the drum envelopes while it is not.
- `NoteResponse`'s FLOW follows the song part, the feeling and the phrase's arc (while the beat is sure), never the loudness fields.
- Then the director's axes.
- Then the macro bank (`core/viz/MacroBank`): the user's global knobs, each an offset on the params it links, `geode_macro_bank_set`.
- A knob may link any float a Customize control sets; the knob editor's Add random and New ranges are native rolls.
- A wave or envelope on a knob (LfoTarget `MACRO`) adds to its turn there, never rewriting it; `geode_macro_bank_live_mods` is the knob's second arc.
- Then LFO, then ADSR.
- An LFO or envelope slot set "On a moment" restarts or fires on Customize › Music's moments (`MomentWatch` in `Reactions`).
- Then `Reactions` (the reaction matrix).
- Then `MotionField`.
- `MotionField`'s bar wave (`uBarOsc`, the breath) follows Rhythm's `barPhase` while the beat is sure and the style's music clock while it is not, blended over a second.
- Then `safety::apply` (`core/viz/VisualSafety`), the unconditional clamp, last.
- In an export the renderer also steps `StyleSchedule` (the segment's style switches, by time and by beat, on the picture's clock, so it holds in silence).
- Then it steps `CameraRig`, the one camera every render path shares, which also reads the sound frame for its own cuts, trauma shake and clock-driven orbit.
- `CameraRig` has twelve paths (`camPath` 0..11): glide, spiral, orbit, hover, drive, fall, rise, weave, corkscrew, swoop, strafe, pendulum.
- The Camera path chips' labels are `camPath`'s `GEODE_PARAM_CHOICES` row in `core/viz/ParamTable.inc`, by index.
- `CameraRig.cpp` static_asserts that row's count (`choiceCount`) against `CameraRig::kPathCount`.
- Every picker's labels (`GEODE_PARAM_CHOICES`) reach the shell as `choice|name|index|label` rows of `geode_params_schema_text` (version 4), read by `ParamSchema.choices`.
- Kaleidoscope is one Float param, `kaleidoFolds` (1 off, 2..16 folds; appended slot 187), a modulation target (`LfoTarget::KaleidoFolds`, `KALEIDO_FOLDS`, ordinal 68, 8 folds per unit) for waves, envelopes, reaction routes and macro knobs.
- The old `kaleidoscope` switch and `symmetry` count are retired rows (`kRetiredParams`); `readParamsJson` turns a look that names them into `kaleidoFolds` (`SceneParams::kaleidoFoldsOf`: off is 1, on its count held to 2..16).
- The shaders fold by the nearest whole count of the modulated `kaleidoFolds`: `SceneParams::kaleidoUniforms` sets `uKaleido`/`uSymmetry` (ShaderScene) and `uPostKaleido`/`uPostSymmetry` (CompositePass), on from 2, the count 6 (`kDefaultSymmetryFolds`) while off.
- A roll turns the kaleidoscope on 3 times in 10, at a count from `SceneParams::kSymmetryFolds` (`core/viz/Params.hpp`).
- Customize's Kaleidoscope slider steps by whole folds (`GeodeNative.sliderIntSteps`) and reads "Off" or the count (`ParamUnit::Folds`, `core/viz/ParamText`).
- Grow and layers (Customize › Shape › Grow): `spawn`, `fractal` (0..1) and `frontLayer`, `backLayer` (-1..1), appended slots 193..196 after the physics layers, default 0, each a modulation target (`SPAWN` .. `BACK_LAYER`, ordinals 74..77, one unit per unit).
- Their reach is the per-style scope `catalogue::Scope::FamilyForm`: the families named in `Catalogue.cpp`'s `kFormFamilies` (Prism, Fluor, Loom, Shard, Petal, Fold).
- Those libraries draw them through `lib_grow` (included by `lib_scene_uniforms`) in their one field function; at the defaults each takes its old path, bit for bit.
- The layers are depth from the eye: `lib_camera`'s `camFly` and `camAround` set it (`growSetup`); an eye wrap (`prismEye` ..) resets the eye (`growEye`).
- `formula` (0..1, slot 197, default 0, `LfoTarget::Formula`, `FORMULA`, ordinal 78) is `uFormula`: which of a style's own distance formulas draws it; it reaches the ids in `kFormulaStyles` (`Scope::Formula`): the sixty styles of Shard, Prism, Fluor, Loom, Petal and Fold.
- Style DNA (`core/viz/ReactionDna`): Shard, Prism, Fluor, Loom, Petal and Fold spend their harmony-or-form route on `LfoTarget::Formula` (a settled section jump, a chord or phrase step, or a slow tension follow, drawn per style), which their styles read as `uFormula`.
- Orbit, hover and pendulum do not travel (`CameraRig::travels`): no flat dive, no bank, no turn-around.
- Strafe looks in at its track from the side; reversing carries the eye round to the other side.
- Corkscrew rolls once per turn; under reduced motion it only leans.

## Visual safety

- Bloom is held to a 0.25 luminance step.
- Brightness, intensity and contrast are held to 1.25.
- The motion params are scaled to 0.4 under reduced motion.
- An LFO on a luminance target runs at 3 Hz at most (`safety::limitLfoRate`).
- A reaction route on a luminance target gets an attack of at least 0.08 s and a release of at least 0.35 s (`Reactions`).
- A cut transition becomes a fade.
- An Add or Difference layer mixes at 0.25 at most.
- Customize › Layers' blends and their labels are `BlendMode` (`core/viz/VisualSafety`, `geode_layer_list`'s BLENDS); the shell holds an index.
- These limit the params only; no pass measures the finished frame: the output flash limiter and its readback are gone.

## Thermal and settling

- The renderer notes settling frames (a staged build, a fresh switch, a transition, a secondary's compiles, a MilkDrop preset load).
- The thermal governor and the fluid scenes' auto quality sit out until 1.5 s after the last settling frame (`ThermalGovernor::noteSettling`), so quick style changes do not read as a slow phone.

## `core/audio`

- `core/audio/ingest/PcmRing` is the lock-free ring each source writes, every block with its rate, int16 and 8-bit made float inside.
- `Cursor` (in `core/audio/ingest/PcmRing`) reads each sample once per reader; a take ends where the rate changes.
- The ingest ABI is `geode_pcm_ring_*`, `geode_pcm_cursor_*`, and `geode_viz_feed_ring` for a live renderer's own cursor.
- `core/audio/ingest/TrackPump` decodes an export's track as each frame asks; its platform decoder is `core/audio/ingest/android/TrackDecoder` (`AMediaExtractor`, `AMediaCodec`, `mediandk`).
- `core/output/ExportFrame` is an export's render order behind `geode_export_job_*` (`core/api/geode_export_job_api.h`): prepare, pre-roll, one call per frame.
- `core/output/LoopRender` is a loop render's whole job behind `geode_loop_job_*` (`core/api/geode_loop_job_api.h`): the encoder ladder, each drift stop's frames, seam, PTS and scratch file.
- `core/output/CastAudioPump` is Cast's sound per video frame behind `geode_cast_audio_*`: the player's ring through its own `Cursor`, the export's `PcmResampler`, the hold and pad of `viz/frame/CastAudio`.
- Its phone host is `core/output/android/LoopRenderHost` (EGL on the encoder's surface, the renderer, the export job, the seam and watermark passes, `MediaCodecSink`'s codec and muxer).
- The loop job runs on one caller thread; `cancel`, `progress` and `state` are safe from another, and native never calls back.
- `core/audio/dsp`'s `DspProcessor` keeps a chain per format, staged at configure and live at flush, so a draining tail never meets the next format's chain.
- `core/audio/dsp` also holds the export resampler `PcmResampler` and the BS.1770 meter `Loudness`.
- `core/audio/dsp/EqPresets` is the built-in equalizer curves; the settings save a curve by its index, so their order is saved data.
- `core/audio/ingest/CapturePlan` orders the formats a capture asks AudioRecord for and sizes its buffer (`geode_capture_plan`, `geode_capture_buffer_bytes`); playback capture only makes the AudioRecord calls.
- `core/audio/ingest/MicInput` is live input: an Oboe input stream on that plan, mono, writing the mic's ring from its callback (`geode_mic_*`); Kotlin only asks the permission and starts and stops it.
- `core/audio/ingest/AiffHeaderScan` (in `AiffReader`) reads an AIFF header pushed to it in small steps, for the export's reader and the player's extractor alike.
- `core/audio/dsp/PlaybackControls` is Settings › Playback's pitch rate and slider step (`core/api/geode_playback_api.cpp`).
- `core/audio/dsp/TrackBookmark` decides whether a track position is remembered: tracks of 20 minutes or more, cleared within 10 s of either end (`core/api/geode_bookmark_api.cpp`).
- Playback is ExoPlayer; the native player (`core/audio/player`) is gone.
- Oboe is back as the output only (`core/audio/output`, off by default).
- `OutputFifo` is the lock-free FIFO between Media3's sink and the device callback: 16-bit to float, underrun count, volume ramp, the position the sink reads.
- `OboeOutput` is the stream, reopened after a route change.
- `OboeOutput` is the one file that includes Oboe, the Prefab AAR `com.google.oboe:oboe`, whose prebuilt `liboboe.so` libgeode links.
- The output's C ABI is `geode_audio_out_*`.
- `RetryBackoff` (`core/audio/output`) is the 60 s window after a failed output stream; `OboeAudioOutput` only reports the failure and asks.
- ExoPlayer keeps decoding, the queue and its whole `DefaultAudioSink`.

## `core/library` and `core/scene/object`

- `core/library` holds TagLib.
- `core/library/Search` is the one library match.
- Search folds text by `SearchFold.inc`, generated by `tools/search_fold.py`.
- Every word must land in title, artist or album.
- A word start ranks above the inside of a word, and title above artist above album.
- Misspellings match only when nothing else matches.
- `SearchIndex` orders and searches the Library screen (`geode_library_index_*`).
- `geode_library_search` answers voice "play X" and Android Auto.
- `core/library/Playlist` reads a picked M3U, PLS or XSPF file (`geode_playlist_parse`); a DOCTYPE in an XSPF is refused.
- Writing playlist files has no caller, so it does not exist.
- `data/PlaylistFormats.kt` only splits the parse text and matches entries to the library by lower-cased file name.
- `core/scene/object` is `geode::scene::object`, #302's object kinds.
- `GltfImport` checks the user's .glb against #455's limits, decimates it with meshoptimizer when up to 4× over, refuses it otherwise, and makes a low-detail copy and its materials.
- `ModelTextures` downscales the model's PNG, JPEG or KTX2 images to the role caps, mipmaps them, encodes to UASTC and transcodes to ASTC 4x4 and ETC2, fitted to the texture memory per model, the simplified mode a later mip base.
- `GltfImport` and `ModelTextures` have no caller or renderer yet.

## Shaders

- Shaders ship in `app/src/main/assets/shaders/`.
- Styles include `lib_*` files by `//#include`, allow-listed in `core/viz/ShaderIncludes.inc`.
- `ShaderSource.cpp` compiles the allow-list in; `tools/check_shaders.py` and `tools/glslcheck` read it.
- 3D styles read the rig through `lib_camera`.
- Every style hears sound through `lib_sound`, pasted by `lib_scene_uniforms`; scenes upload it with `core/viz/SoundUniforms`.
- A style's six knobs are `uKnob[6]` in `lib_scene_uniforms` (k0 PULSE … k5 SPACE).
- `ShaderScene::uploadParams` takes the knobs from `NoteResponse` (exactly 0.5 while it is off).
- The neon set (`glow_vortex`, `neon_chrome_sea`, `black_hole`, `hex_hive`, `mandelbox_cathedral`) shares `lib_neon`.
- Each neon style spends Morph, Warp, Turbulence and Intensity on its own geometry through normalized reads, so Customize, the macro bank and DNA reach it, and adds each style knob's offset from 0.5 on top.
- `lib_shell` maps a style's normalized inputs (0..1, 0.5 = the tuned constant) to their ranges (`shellMap`) and applies Spawn, Fractal and the two layers to a style's own field (`SHELL_FIELD3`, `SHELL_DENSITY3`, `SHELL_FIELD2`); the hooks are 0.0 until the engine uploads them.
- Silk and Morphing (ids `acid_*`, `AcidScene`) present their field as a lit relief through `lib_relief`.
- Life presents its field in 3D through `core/viz/present/FieldPresent` (`life_show_frag`): seven styles a terrain on the rig, Gyre a torus, Pulsar and Mitosis a sphere.
- `lib_relief` gives parallax, a sound-driven light and tilt, and eight material looks taken from the families.
- `core/viz/present/FieldPresent` presents a simulation in 3D: `field_prep_frag` writes its state into a mipmapped field (height, tint, age), and a `ShaderScene` fed that field (`ShaderScene::Field`) draws it, so the present reads the whole shared block like a style.
- That present shader includes `lib_field3d`, defines its colours, then `lib_field3d_march`: the field marched as a terrain on `camFly` or a torus or sphere on `camAround`, with finer copies of the field as fractal detail.
- In it Kaleidoscope, Twist, Tile, Warp and Ripple bend the 3D shape (`VIEW_BENDS_IN_3D` keeps them out of `view()`).
- `core/viz/present/FieldGeometry` is its pure maths: each surface's size and eye, and where a finger lands on the field (`field_present_test`).
- The composite applies no geometry, mirror or grade to a scene that draws the rig itself (`grade::gateFor`, `Scene::drawsCamera`).
- A held simulation that draws the rig redraws its held field when the rig moves (`Scene::redrawHeld`); it is not stepped.
- A style family's styles share `lib_<family>` and are listed in `core/viz/Catalogue.cpp`; a family carries its own count.
- Flame, Opal, Hollows, Prism, Fluor, Loom, Shard and Petal also include `lib_<family>_march`, after the style's own map: no style declares a function ahead of its body.
- Glass, Nebula, Terra and Truchet include `lib_<family>_field` after the style's pure map (`glassMap`, `nebMap`, `terraMap`, `truchetMap`); its `<family>Field` is the one entry the march, normal and shading read, and writes no global.
- Foam's one field entry is `foamField` in `lib_foam`; it returns a `FoamSample` and writes no global.
- Foam, Glass, Nebula, Terra and Truchet read the params and the six knobs once, normalized, in `<family>In()` (the normalization shell, at rest exactly the tuned constants); `<FAMILY>_GROW` and `<FAMILY>_FORMULA` are the one-line hooks for lib_grow and uFormula.
- Fold's styles include one of five marches after their map instead, one to a kind of fractal: `lib_fold_march_glow`, `_cone`, `_slice`, `_pair` and `_probe`.
- Each Fold march includes `lib_fold_at` first: `foldAt`, the one read of the style's map (`FOLD_AT`), with the base, Spawn and Fractal passes in one loop so the fractal compiles once per read.
- Shard, Prism and Fluor also include `lib_strata` before `lib_<family>`: a far fractal lattice and a domain-warped haze round the marched form, and the folds, sponge and nested shells a style's form is built from.
- `uFormula` (lib_scene_uniforms) picks one of three formulas in a Shard, Prism or Fluor style, crossfaded in two narrow bands (`strataFormula`); 0 is each style's original geometry.
- Loom, Petal and Fold include `lib_layers` at the end of their library: a domain-warped fractal haze in front of the style's form and a far fractal behind it.
- `lib_layers` also gives those styles three formulas each, picked by `uFormula` (Formula, 0..1) with a narrow crossfade between neighbours.
- `lib_layers`' `gLayerIter` grows a fractal's fractional iteration count with Detail (`uSteps`), k1 TEXTURE and k4 BUILD.
- Fractal (`uFractal`) also grows `gLayerIter` into its headroom (never past 1) and adds up to two folds to `gStrataIters` and one and a half to `gStrataFarIters`; at 0 both are unchanged.
- Every style of the families from Flame on marches and flies the rig, by family name (`kMarchedFamilies` in `core/viz/Catalogue.cpp`); none has a spin rate.
- A family's styles are listed by id, never matched by prefix: `prism_rain` is a loose style, not one of the Prism family.
- Twelve styles each: `arbor_*`, `bulb_*`, `chrys_*`, `lucid_*`, `dive_*`, `glass_*`, `nebula_*`, `terra_*`, `truchet_*`, `foam_*`.
- Ten styles each: `flame_*`, `opal_*`, `hollow_*`, `prism_*`, `fluor_*`, `loom_*`, `shard_*`, `petal_*`, `fold_*`.

## Physics layers

- The physics layers (`core/viz/physics`) lay a fluid and particles over the finished picture of any style, after Trails, Liquid and the dive and before the composite.
- Their order is back fluid | style | front particles.
- The fluid is a low-res `fluid::FluidSim`: the picture's contours stir its velocity and its lit parts pour their colour into its ink (`physics_seed_frag`, `FluidSim::applyField`).
- The fluid sits behind the style (filling its darks) or is screened over it, by `simLayerOrder` (`physics_layer_frag`).
- The particles are `fluid::Particles` riding the fluid's velocity, or their own curl drift with the fluid off, screened on and coloured from the picture under them (`physics_particle_vert`/`_frag`).
- Their params are `simFluid`, `simParticles`, `simForce` and `simLayerOrder` (slots 189..192), LfoTargets `SIM_FLUID` .. `SIM_LAYER_ORDER` (ordinals 70..73), on every style.
- Both mixes are 0 in every look, so a look draws as before and the pass makes no GL call; no roll deals them.
- The music reaches them only through waves, envelopes, reaction routes and macro knobs; nothing in the layers hears the sound.
- The two mixes and the fluid's place are luminance targets (3 Hz, route floors); reduced motion slows the force (`safety::apply`).
- The plan (`PhysicsPlan`): FluidQuality's Low grids on a cool phone, its Min grids at Reduced, no fluid at Minimal; an export is always Full.
- Grids, particles and the pass's target are made when a layer turns on (live one part a frame, an export whole) and freed 120 frames after it goes off.
- They step on the music clock: silence holds them, an export's fixed step replays them.
- During a style transition the same layers are laid over the outgoing style too, so they hold through the fade.

## Endless zoom, Trails, Liquid

- Endless zoom is a stage of its own (`core/viz/EndlessZoom.hpp`, `core/viz/DivePass`, `dive_frag`).
- It is drawn over the finished picture of every style kind (flat and 3D shaders, MilkDrop, simulations, particles), after Trails or Liquid and before the composite.
- It shows the picture nested inside itself (`endless::tunnel`), an octave apart at Ring spacing 1.
- Every copy grows at Dive speed (`endlessZoomSpeed`, signed: in falls, out rises) and dissolves into the next over a soft rim.
- The rim has no outline, shadow or mirrored border: `viz_math_test` fails on a ridge.
- A new copy opens at the aim point; nothing is fed back, so the style stays live.
- Customize › Dive shapes it: `diveSpacing`, `diveShape`, `diveBlend`, `diveFog`, `diveLens`, `diveTwist`, `diveAimX/Y`.
- `diveFollow` sets how much of the music's follow (below) it takes.
- Nothing else moves it: no style Zoom, camera, Director, macro, Note & key or reaction, and no hit, band or beat.
- `endless::Follow` is the music's only hand on it: the absolute `drive` averaged over the last 8 beats `Rhythm` measured (never a tempo it did not measure), worth ±15 %, off without a locked beat.
- It stops in silence like everything else: no sound, no movement.
- The renderer's step is the frame's times `sound::motionRate`, 0 once `presence` has faded.
- Slider glides and style transitions keep wall time.
- Simulation, particle and MilkDrop styles hold their last frame in silence; an export holds the same way (`export_preroll_test`).
- A flat style's Travel zoom (`CameraRig::flatDive`) travels the same stage; `CameraRig` no longer flies for Endless zoom.
- A shader style's Trails are MilkDrop's feedback through `LiquidPass` with no flow (`Renderer::drawTrails`).
- Trails paint the whole frame, so nothing kept in the scene target survives.
- Trail length is the feedback's sustain (`endless::feedback::sustainInject`).
- Trail zoom is its video echo and Trail warp its warp (`motion::trailBase`), on flat and 3D shader styles alike.
- Liquid mode is `SceneParams.flowEnabled`, Customize › Advanced › Liquid.
- Liquidity, Push, Swirl and Dive are `flowStrength`, `flowForce`, `flowCurl`, `endlessZoomSpeed`, also LFO targets.
- In Liquid mode a flat style dives by the liquid's own wandering feedback dive (`liquid::Dive`, inward only, at Dive speed times the same slow follow).
- In Liquid mode a 3D style dives by the endless zoom stage.
- In Liquid mode touch moves the liquid.
- In Liquid mode a hot phone follows Settings › Performance.
- `core/viz/frame/Quality` is Settings › Performance's tiers, scale choices, preset match and first-run pick (`core/api/geode_perf_api.cpp`).

## UI blur

- The UI blur (`core/viz/UiBlurPass`, `ui_blur_frag` and `ui_blur_mask_frag`) frosts the live visual under the app's glass sheet after the composite.
- Its region is a rounded rect set by `StageView.setUiBlurRegion` (0..1, y down; `geode_viz_set_ui_blur_region`).
- It blurs at quarter resolution, is free at strength 0 and is never drawn offscreen.
