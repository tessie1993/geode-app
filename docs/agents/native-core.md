# Native core (`core/`, `libgeode.so`)

## Build and ABI

- The root `CMakeLists.txt` builds the native core, plus `core/CMakeLists.txt` for the `geode_core` static library.
- `app/src/main/cpp/*_jni.cpp` only marshal values into the `extern "C"` ABI in `core/api/geode_api.h`.
- projectM gets `tools/projectm-v4.1.7-render-fbo-backport.patch` at configure time.
- projectM also gets `tools/projectm-v4.1.7-shader-fallback.patch`, which reports the shaders it swapped for the default (`projectm_get_dropped_shaders`).
- The submodules are `third_party/{projectm,taglib,cgltf,meshoptimizer,basis_universal,kissfft,libdatachannel,mbedtls}`.
- libdatachannel and its Mbed TLS (`core/cast/mbedtls_user_config.h`) build only for `core/cast/WebRtcLink`, Show on a computer's WebRTC; `core/cast/` is the only native directory that may include them or a socket header (`tools/network-gate.sh`).
- `core/cast/LiveServer` is the cast's HTTP server on plain POSIX sockets, bound to the phone's LAN address; `LiveHttp` (requests and answers) and `LiveSegments` (the segment window and playlist) hold its decisions and include no socket header.
- KISS FFT's header-only `kissfft.hh` is the transform under `core/sound/Fft`.
- cgltf is compiled in `GltfImport.cpp`.
- meshoptimizer's simplifier and codecs build as `geode_meshopt`, in `core/CMakeLists.txt`.
- Basis Universal's encoder, transcoder and zstd build as `geode_basisu`, in `core/CMakeLists.txt`.

## `core/sound`: hearing

- `Silence` is the wallpaper's and dream's rest-when-silent rule, polled through `geode_silence_*`.
- The sound engine is MilkDrop's FFT path beside a TouchDesigner-style per-sample filter bank, stepped once per rendered frame into a `GeodeSoundFrame`.
- How much the music is comes from absolute loudness, never from the song's own average.
- `LufsMeter` (fosfora's) gives BS.1770 momentary and short-term LUFS (`loudnessM`/`loudnessS`) and the one silence gate every detector reads.
- `busy` is onset and drum density, the beat.
- `drive` = loudness × texture.
- `sound/DriveGain` turns `drive` into the gain that `MotionField`, `CameraRig`, `NoteResponse` and `Reactions` multiply the magnitude of audio-driven motion by.
- The relative bass/mid/treble ratios only shape the pulse.
- Settings › Audio's input trim is the analysis-only gain for a mic or another app's audio.
- A live renderer hears the player only on a ring a sink plays (`sound::player::heardAsPlayer`, set by `geode_viz_feed_ring`); the wallpaper's output mix hears no player.

## `core/sound`: the musical layer

- `Rhythm`: tempo, beat, bar, phrase, on a fixed 10 ms hop of the written samples.
- `Harmony`: chroma, key, chord, tension, scale from `ScaleTable.inc`.
- `ScaleTable.inc` is generated by `tools/musical_intelligence.py` with `docs/musical-intelligence/`.
- `Form`: novelty, sections, build-up, drop, section state.
- `Emotion` reads the frame as a feeling: valence, energy, tension and GEMS-9, from `EmotionTable.inc`.
- `EmotionTable.inc` holds a mode vector per scale and a key character per key, generated by `tools/music_emotion.py`.

## `core/viz`: renderer, scenes, catalogue

- `core/viz` holds the renderer, the scene registry, `scenes/`, `fluid/`, `liquid/` and `compute/`.
- `Catalogue` is the style catalogue: every style id, kind, family, param scope and control range.
- Kotlin reads the catalogue as one text table, `geode_catalogue_text`.
- The catalogue's presets section, `CataloguePresets`, is the bundled looks by param name; `data/BuiltInPresets` reads it through `geode_catalogue_presets_text`.
- `Palette` (`core/viz/Palette`) is Customize › Look's palette stack, the one colour source to be; no renderer reads it yet.
- A stack holds up to 8 palettes of up to 16 colours, each Smooth or Steps, Mirror or Loop, RGB, hue or OKLab, a place per colour and one colour's Shades, Flat or To black; Share (Soft or Hard edges) or Mix by amount, full range off.
- Its library is `PaletteTable.inc` (old `palette` ramps 0..20, colour maps 21..25, then newer); it also reads an old look's colours as a stack and keeps the picker's recent colours; text in, text out, `geode_palette_*`.
- How much each palette is used and where along the stack the colours sit are scene params (`paletteAmount0..7`, `paletteShift`, read by `palette::amountsOf`), so waves, envelopes, routes and the macro bank reach them; LFO targets `PALETTE_AMOUNT_1`, `_3`..`_8` and `PALETTE_SHIFT` (luminance targets: 3 Hz, the route floors). Palette 2's amount takes `PALETTE_MIX` once the renderer draws the stack.
- `fluid/` is the stable-fluids solver: MacCormack advection, batched splats, a multigrid pressure solve, heat and buoyancy.
- The fluid solver's pure maths is `FluidMath`.
- `FlowField` is the velocity field Liquid mode reads, kicked by touch and drops.
- The touch smear (`core/viz/TouchSmear`, Settings › Touch › Smear): touch play's fingers kick the shared `FlowField` and the composite bends the finished picture by it (`uFlow`).
- The smear runs with Liquid off, live only, as an optional pass (`geode_viz_set_touch_smear`).
- `liquid/LiquidMath` is Liquid mode's pure maths: thermal modes, inject weight, the random endless dive, gestures, born clock.
- `liquid/FeedbackMotion` is MilkDrop's per-frame UV transform (zoom, zoomexp, stretch, warp, rotation about a centre, translation) made per second, the sound's push on it, the mirror fold, and what the renderer asks of it (`TrailAsk`, `fromDive`).
- `LiquidPass` is a full-resolution feedback ping-pong between the scene target and the composite.
- `LiquidPass` samples the last frame through that transform (`lib_feedback`, the same maths in GLSL), then the `FlowField` velocity, folds it back at the edges, and re-pours the style through `liquid_frag`.
- Expert › Shader code shows on Fluid only (`core/viz/CustomizeMap`, `Show::ShaderCode`), with the custom force and dye editors; a shader style has no box for its own fragment shader any more.
- A look's saved custom shader still applies (`core/viz/ShaderOverrides`, `geode_shader_override_*`).
- Your colours are native (`core/viz/Colour.hpp`, `geode_colour_*`, `geode_palette_*`): up to five stops, scene params `paletteStop0..4` (packed 0xRRGGBB), that loop seamlessly; shaders read them through `lib_palette`'s `palStops`.
- `core/viz/RandomizeAll` (`geode_randomize_all_*`) is the two-circle gesture's Randomize everything.
- `core/viz/Shake` and `core/viz/TapAlong` decide the easter eggs: a firm shake, four taps on the heard beat.

## Per-frame chain

The renderer runs these in order each frame.

- `Director`, the auto-director: at sections and drops the feeling decides a behaviour look, held until the next.
- The director's five axes are Energy, Speed, Complexity, Space and Chaos: offsets on scene params and the camera's music moves; never the user's macro bank.
- The director also sets the camera's softness and the motion dials by a factor; a dial at 0 stays off.
- The director is base plus modulation, MilkDrop's rule.
- The director's axis push scales into the room a slider has left above its default, so a slider between its default and the end still moves the picture (`director::applyAxes`).
- Owner's rule: the director never touches a colour param and plays no route of its own, and nothing in it follows the frame.
- `DirectorReach` is the director's reach past the look when Visuals › Settings asks.
- Before the look, the reach shuffles, and the Customize randomizer rolls some categories at a section, drop or long stretch from the play seed; sliders glide and toggles flip on the moment.
- After its axes, the reach adds three slow waves and three sound followers of its own.
- All reach offsets sit over the user's params and keep off kept controls, the style's scope, a control the user just moved and the hand mask.
- `NoteResponse` (Note & key reactions): notes, chords, key and mood read into the six style knobs only, drums weighted by measured percussiveness.
- `NoteResponse` writes no scene param and no colour, so what moves on a note, chord or a drop is the reaction matrix alone.
- Then the director's axes.
- Then the macro bank (`core/viz/MacroBank`): the user's global knobs, each an offset on the params it links, `geode_macro_bank_set`.
- A knob may link any float a Customize control sets; the knob editor's Add random and New ranges are native rolls.
- A wave or envelope on a knob (LfoTarget `MACRO`) adds to its turn there, never rewriting it; `geode_macro_bank_live_mods` is the knob's second arc.
- Then LFO, then ADSR.
- An LFO or envelope slot set "On a moment" restarts or fires on Customize › Music's moments (`MomentWatch` in `Reactions`).
- Then `Reactions` (the reaction matrix).
- Then `MotionField`.
- Then `safety::apply` (`core/viz/VisualSafety`), the unconditional clamp, last.
- In an export the renderer also steps `StyleSchedule` (the segment's style switches, by time and by beat, on the picture's clock, so it holds in silence).
- Then it steps `CameraRig`, the one camera every render path shares, which also reads the sound frame for its own cuts, trauma shake and clock-driven orbit.

## Visual safety

- Bloom is held to a 0.25 luminance step.
- Brightness, intensity and contrast are held to 1.25.
- The motion params are scaled to 0.4 under reduced motion.
- An LFO on a luminance target runs at 3 Hz at most (`safety::limitLfoRate`).
- A reaction route on a luminance target gets an attack of at least 0.08 s and a release of at least 0.35 s (`Reactions`).
- A cut transition becomes a fade.
- An Add or Difference layer mixes at 0.25 at most.
- These limit the params only; no pass measures the finished frame: the output flash limiter and its readback are gone.

## Thermal and settling

- The renderer notes settling frames (a staged build, a fresh switch, a transition, a secondary's compiles, a MilkDrop preset load).
- The thermal governor and the fluid scenes' auto quality sit out until 1.5 s after the last settling frame (`ThermalGovernor::noteSettling`), so quick style changes do not read as a slow phone.

## `core/audio`

- `core/audio/ingest/PcmRing` is the lock-free ring each source writes, every block with its rate, int16 and 8-bit made float inside.
- `Cursor` (in `core/audio/ingest/PcmRing`) reads each sample once per reader; a take ends where the rate changes.
- The ingest ABI is `geode_pcm_ring_*`, `geode_pcm_cursor_*`, and `geode_viz_feed_ring` for a live renderer's own cursor.
- `core/audio/dsp`'s `DspProcessor` keeps a chain per format, staged at configure and live at flush, so a draining tail never meets the next format's chain.
- `core/audio/dsp` also holds the export resampler `PcmResampler` and the BS.1770 meter `Loudness`.
- Playback is ExoPlayer; the native player (`core/audio/player`) is gone.
- Oboe is back as the output only (`core/audio/output`, off by default).
- `OutputFifo` is the lock-free FIFO between Media3's sink and the device callback: 16-bit to float, underrun count, volume ramp, the position the sink reads.
- `OboeOutput` is the stream, reopened after a route change.
- `OboeOutput` is the one file that includes Oboe, the Prefab AAR `com.google.oboe:oboe`, whose prebuilt `liboboe.so` libgeode links.
- The output's C ABI is `geode_audio_out_*`.
- ExoPlayer keeps decoding, the queue and its whole `DefaultAudioSink`.

## `core/library` and `core/scene/object`

- `core/library` holds TagLib.
- `core/library/Search` is the one library match.
- Search folds text by `SearchFold.inc`, generated by `tools/search_fold.py`.
- Every word must land in title, artist or album.
- A word start ranks above the inside of a word, and title above artist above album.
- Misspellings match only when nothing else matches.
- `SearchIndex` orders and searches the Library screen (`geode_library_index_*`).
- `geode_library_search` answers voice "play X" and Android Auto.
- `core/scene/object` is `geode::scene::object`, #302's object kinds.
- `GltfImport` checks the user's .glb against #455's limits, decimates it with meshoptimizer when up to 4× over, refuses it otherwise, and makes a low-detail copy and its materials.
- `ModelTextures` downscales the model's PNG, JPEG or KTX2 images to the role caps, mipmaps them, encodes to UASTC and transcodes to ASTC 4x4 and ETC2, fitted to the texture memory per model, the simplified mode a later mip base.
- `GltfImport` and `ModelTextures` have no caller or renderer yet.

## Shaders

- Shaders ship in `app/src/main/assets/shaders/`.
- Styles include `lib_*` files by `//#include`, allow-listed in `core/viz/ShaderIncludes.inc`.
- `ShaderSource.cpp` compiles the allow-list in; `tools/check_shaders.py` and `tools/glslcheck` read it.
- 3D styles read the rig through `lib_camera`.
- Every style hears sound through `lib_sound`, pasted by `lib_scene_uniforms`; scenes upload it with `core/viz/SoundUniforms`.
- A style's six knobs are `uKnob[6]` in `lib_scene_uniforms` (k0 PULSE … k5 SPACE).
- `ShaderScene::uploadParams` takes the knobs from `NoteResponse` (exactly 0.5 while it is off).
- The neon set (`glow_vortex`, `neon_chrome_sea`, `black_hole`, `hex_hive`, `mandelbox_cathedral`) shares `lib_neon`.
- Each neon style spends Morph, Warp, Turbulence and Intensity on its own geometry through normalized reads, so Customize, the macro bank and DNA reach it, and adds each style knob's offset from 0.5 on top.
- The simulation scenes (Silk, Fluid, Life, Morphing (ids `acid_*`, `AcidScene`), Cymatics) present their field as a lit 3D surface through `lib_relief`.
- `lib_relief` gives parallax, a sound-driven light and tilt, and eight material looks taken from the families.
- A style family's styles share `lib_<family>` and are listed in `core/viz/Catalogue.cpp`; a family carries its own count.
- Flame, Opal and Hollows also include `lib_<family>_march`, after the style's own map: no style declares a function ahead of its body.
- Twelve styles each: `arbor_*`, `bulb_*`, `chrys_*`, `lucid_*`, `dive_*`, `glass_*`, `nebula_*`, `terra_*`, `truchet_*`, `foam_*`.
- Ten styles each: `flame_*`, `opal_*`, `hollow_*`.

## Endless zoom, Trails, Liquid

- Endless zoom is a stage of its own (`core/viz/EndlessZoom.hpp`, `core/viz/DivePass`, `dive_frag`).
- It is drawn over the finished picture of every style kind (flat and 3D shaders, MilkDrop, simulations, particles), after Trails or Liquid and before the composite.
- It shows the picture nested inside itself (`endless::tunnel`), an octave apart at Ring spacing 1.
- Every copy grows at Dive speed (`endlessZoomSpeed`, signed: in falls, out rises) and dissolves into the next over a soft rim.
- The rim has no outline, shadow or mirrored border: `viz_math_test` fails on a ridge.
- A new copy opens at the aim point; nothing is fed back, so the style stays live.
- Customize › Dive shapes it: `diveSpacing`, `diveShape`, `diveBlend`, `diveFog`, `diveLens`, `diveTwist`, `diveAimX/Y`.
- `diveFollow` sets how much of the music's follow (below) it takes.
- Nothing else moves it: no style Zoom, camera, Director, macro, Note & key or reaction, and no hit, band or beat.
- `endless::Follow` is the music's only hand on it: the absolute `drive` averaged over the last 8 beats `Rhythm` measured (never a tempo it did not measure), worth ±15 %, off without a locked beat.
- It stops in silence like everything else: no sound, no movement.
- The renderer's step is the frame's times `sound::motionRate`, 0 once `presence` has faded.
- Slider glides and style transitions keep wall time.
- Simulation, particle and MilkDrop styles hold their last frame in silence; an export holds the same way (`export_preroll_test`).
- A flat style's Travel zoom (`CameraRig::flatDive`) travels the same stage; `CameraRig` no longer flies for Endless zoom.
- A shader style's Trails are MilkDrop's feedback through `LiquidPass` with no flow (`Renderer::drawTrails`).
- Trails paint the whole frame, so nothing kept in the scene target survives.
- Trail length is the feedback's sustain (`endless::feedback::sustainInject`).
- Trail zoom is its video echo and Trail warp its warp (`motion::trailBase`), on flat and 3D shader styles alike.
- Liquid mode is `SceneParams.flowEnabled`, Customize › Advanced › Liquid.
- Liquidity, Push, Swirl and Dive are `flowStrength`, `flowForce`, `flowCurl`, `endlessZoomSpeed`, also LFO targets.
- In Liquid mode a flat style dives by the liquid's own wandering feedback dive (`liquid::Dive`, inward only, at Dive speed times the same slow follow).
- In Liquid mode a 3D style dives by the endless zoom stage.
- In Liquid mode touch moves the liquid.
- In Liquid mode a hot phone follows Settings › Performance.

## UI blur

- The UI blur (`core/viz/UiBlurPass`, `ui_blur_frag` and `ui_blur_mask_frag`) frosts the live visual under the app's glass sheet after the composite.
- Its region is a rounded rect set by `VisualizerView.setUiBlurRegion` (0..1, y down; `geode_viz_set_ui_blur_region`).
- It blurs at quarter resolution, is free at strength 0 and is never drawn offscreen.
