# Pre-release app audit (2026-11-01)

Four read-only reviews of `claude/geode-finish` (crash/ANR/leak, security/privacy/Play policy,
performance/memory/battery, completeness/UX/accessibility). Findings already fixed in open PRs
(#197 emulator ANR, #199 release bug fixes) are left out. "Suspect" means not confirmed in code.

Who: **Code** = can be fixed in a PR; **You** = a product decision first.

## Suspect (verify)

- Glance 1.1.1 → WorkManager may merge `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`/`WAKE_LOCK` not named in the privacy policy (check CI's merged manifest).
- `PlaybackService.ensureRunning`: `startForegroundService` from background is refused on Android 12+ (caught), so a resume after a call may play without the FGS.
- Old session's `drainStoreWrites` can overlap a new `PlayerSession`'s writes after back-then-relaunch.
