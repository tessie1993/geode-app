MuzViz Studio Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

MuzViz Studio Pro ("MuzViz", "the app") is a music player, music visualizer and music video studio for Android. This policy describes what the app does with your information. The short version: MuzViz collects nothing, sends nothing and has no servers. The one exception is buying MuzViz Pro, which Google Play handles.

Information the app does not collect

MuzViz does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. The app has:

  • no user accounts, sign-in or registration;
  • no analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs;
  • no advertising identifier;
  • no INTERNET permission. The app cannot send your data anywhere.

Buying MuzViz Pro (Google Play)

MuzViz Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through Google Play Billing. When you buy it, Google Play handles the whole payment: your payment method, billing address and the order are processed by Google under the Google Payments privacy notice and the Google Privacy Policy. MuzViz never sees your payment details.

To unlock Pro, the app asks the Google Play Store app on your device which purchases your Google account owns. Play returns a purchase record (product, purchase time, order ID and a purchase token). The app checks its signature on the device and confirms ("acknowledges") the purchase back to Google Play, as Google Play requires. It keeps only a yes/no "Pro owned" flag and the time it last checked. The purchase record isn't stored or sent anywhere else. This exchange goes between MuzViz and the Play Store app on your device; MuzViz itself still makes no internet connection.

The free one-hour trial of Pro runs entirely on your device: the app stores when it started, and nothing else.

Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13+) / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): to find and play the music on your device.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: only for the optional Live input and Visualize other apps features (Android requires it for its playback-capture API). Requested when you turn one of them on, never at launch.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): playback controls, and the ongoing notifications Android requires while Visualize other apps or a video render runs.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE with the media-playback, media-projection, media-processing and data-sync types: lets playback, capture and video rendering continue while the app is not on screen, always with a visible notification.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets the app talk to the Google Play Store app to sell and restore MuzViz Pro.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: for bit-perfect USB audio output to an external DAC.
  • VIBRATE: haptic feedback for the app's controls.

MuzViz requests no camera, contacts, location or internet permission.

Visualize other apps (playback capture)

If you turn this on, Android shows its own consent dialog first and an ongoing notification for as long as it runs. The device's audio output is read only to animate the visuals. It passes through an in-memory buffer and is never saved, recorded or sent.

Now playing (notification access)

Optionally, you can give MuzViz notification access so it can show which app and track is playing elsewhere. It reads only the media sessions Android exposes through that access; notification contents are not read, stored or sent. You can revoke it in system settings at any time.

Video export

Exported videos are MP4 files saved where you choose. They stay on your device unless you share them; the app you share to handles them under its own policy. Free-tier exports carry a small "MuzViz Studio Pro" mark in the corner.

Backups

If Android backup is on, your MuzViz settings, presets and playlists may be included in your Google account backup, handled by Google. The analysis cache and crash file are excluded. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Children

MuzViz is not directed at children under 13. It contains no ads, no chat and no content from other users, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything MuzViz stores lives in its private storage. Uninstalling the app, or Settings → Apps → MuzViz Studio Pro → Storage → Clear data, removes it. Your Pro purchase stays with your Google account and is restored automatically after reinstalling. Exported videos are ordinary files and are deleted like any other file.

Third-party components

MuzViz bundles open-source libraries (including libprojectM, AndroidX/Jetpack and Media3) and the Google Play Billing Library. None of them collect or transmit data in this app, apart from the Play purchase exchange described above. The open-source notices are in the app under Settings.
