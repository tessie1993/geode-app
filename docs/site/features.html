01 · Visuals that listen

It hears the song, not just the volume

The visuals react to the sound itself, as it plays:

  • Loudness, bass, mids and treble
  • Drum hits: kick, snare and hats
  • Notes, chords and the key of the song
  • The build-ups and the drops

No sound, no visuals: when the music stops, the picture comes to rest. The visuals can also follow the microphone or another app's sound, if you turn that on.

02 · 334 styles

Fly through it, fall into it

3D scenes you fly through, fractals, nebulae, terrain, glass, fluid and particle simulations, in nineteen families. More than 700 bundled looks on top, and with Pro, your own .milk preset files.

Screenshots of the app on a phone, playing music.

03 · Auto mode

A director that follows the song

Auto mode changes the style, the camera and the motion as the song moves from one part to the next. Your colours stay yours: it never narrows the palette you picked.

Free the first minute of each song Pro the whole song

04 · Customize

Make every visual yours

Start with one knob, or go eleven groups deep. Everything you change can be saved, shared and brought back.

  • Four quick knobs. Glow, Heat, Bend and Flow change the whole feel with one turn.
  • Knobs of your own Pro. Link any parameters to a knob, shape it with a curve (ease in, ease out, S-curve) and let a wave or an envelope turn it.
  • You choose what reacts. See and edit what moves on a kick, a note or a drop; shuffle it, or go back to the style's own.
  • Eleven groups deep. Camera, motion, the endless dive, effects, transitions and more.
  • Your colours. Take the palette from the album artwork, or pick your own.
  • Touch it. Pinch and twist the picture, smear it with your fingers, or push the liquid mode around.
  • Presets and templates. Save a look, take parts from another, share it as a template, and open shared ones.
  • Randomize everything Pro, then keep what you like.

Questions

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is one in-app purchase through Google Play. You can try it for one hour first.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No account, ever. The app works offline. The internet is used only for Cast to TV and for showing the visual on a computer, over your own Wi-Fi. The Google Cast component sends Google anonymous usage logs; the privacy policy explains this.

What does it ask permission for, and why?

Each one is asked for when you first need it, with the reason first.

  • Music and audio: to find and play the music on your phone, the first time you open Library.
  • Notifications: playback controls, and progress while a video renders or a cast runs.
  • Microphone (optional): only if you let the visuals follow the room, another app's sound, or the wallpaper hear other apps. The sound is analysed on the phone and never sent anywhere.
  • Screen and audio capture (optional): Android's own dialog, each time you start visualizing another app.
  • Notification access (optional): to show the name of a song another app is playing. Only media sessions are read.
  • Network: only for Cast to TV and showing the visual on a computer, on your own Wi-Fi.

Never asked for: camera, location, contacts, phone, SMS or all-files access.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 13 or newer.

Where do my videos go?

Into your phone's Movies folder. Nothing you make leaves the phone unless you share it.

How do I get help?

Email CONTACT_EMAIL. Say which phone and Android version you have, and what you were doing.

Photosensitivity warning

The visuals are bright, fast and full-screen, and they can flash. A small number of people can have a seizure when they see flashing lights or patterns, even with no history of epilepsy. The app limits how far and how fast its own effects may change the brightness, and it follows Android's "Remove animations" setting, but it does not measure the finished picture, and .milk presets only get a brightness cap: the flashes they draw themselves are not limited. It cannot make the visuals safe for everyone. If you or anyone watching is sensitive to flashing light, do not use the visuals, and stop at once if you feel unwell.
