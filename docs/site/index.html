Your music, made visible

This is the app's own start-up clip. Inside the app, every style flies, folds and glows in time with what is playing. When the music stops, the picture comes to rest.

What it does

One app to play your music, watch it and turn it into a video. See every feature, and what Free and Pro include.

Play your music

  • Tracks, albums, artists, folders and playlists, with search
  • Queue, shuffle, repeat, sleep timer, ReplayGain, equalizer, fades
  • Lyrics from an .lrc file or the track's tags
  • Home screen widget and Quick Settings tile

Watch it

  • 3D scenes you fly through, fractals, nebulae, terrain, glass, fluid and particles
  • Auto mode changes style, camera and motion as the song moves on
  • Customize everything, or push the liquid mode around with your fingers
  • More than 700 bundled looks; save and share your own

Make a video

  • Render the visual over a song to an MP4 on your phone
  • 720p, 1080p or 4K, 24 to 60 frames per second, H.264 or HEVC
  • Widescreen, vertical, square and more
  • Record a live take of what the visual shows and hears (Pro)

Beyond the phone

  • Live wallpaper and screen saver (Pro)
  • Cast to a TV with Google Cast (Pro)
  • Show it in a browser on a computer on your Wi-Fi (Pro)
  • HDMI, USB-C or wireless displays (Pro)

Free and Pro

No ads in either. Pro is a single in-app purchase, not a subscription, and you can try it for one hour first.

Free

  • The full music player
  • More than 60 styles, the first of every family
  • The basic controls in Customize
  • Video up to 1080p at 30 fps, one minute, with a watermark
  • Auto mode directs the first minute of each song

Pro

  • Every style and every control, your own knobs
  • 4K and 60 fps, full length, no watermark
  • Loop and playlist renders, and Record
  • Wallpaper, screen saver, Cast and external displays
  • Auto mode for the whole song

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your music, your listening history, your settings and the videos you make stay on your phone. The internet is used only for Cast to TV and for showing the visual on a computer, over your own Wi-Fi. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from anywhere else.

Read the privacy policy

Photosensitivity warning

The visuals are bright, fast and full-screen, and they can flash. A small number of people can have a seizure when they see flashing lights or patterns, even with no history of epilepsy. The app limits how far and how fast its own effects change the brightness and follows Android's "Remove animations" setting, but it cannot make the visuals safe for everyone. If you or anyone watching is sensitive to flashing light, do not use the visuals, and stop at once if you feel unwell.
