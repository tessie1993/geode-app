Customize

Make every visual yours

Start with a single knob, or go eleven groups deep. Everything you change can be saved, shared and brought back.

  • Four quick knobsGlow, Heat, Bend and Flow change the whole feel with one turn.
  • Knobs of your own ProLink any parameters to a knob, shape it with a curve, and let a wave or an envelope turn it for you.
  • You choose what reactsSee and edit what moves on a kick, a note or a drop. Shuffle it, or go back to the style's own.
  • Your coloursTake the palette from the album artwork or pick your own. Auto mode never narrows it.
  • Touch itPinch and twist the picture, smear it with your fingers, or push the liquid mode around.
  • Presets and templatesSave any look, borrow parts from another, share it as a template. 700+ looks built in, and your own .milk presets with Pro.

Privacy

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your music, history, settings and videos stay on your phone. The internet is used only for Cast to TV and for showing the visual on a computer, over your own Wi-Fi. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from anywhere else.

Read the privacy policy

Photosensitivity warning

The visuals are bright, fast and full-screen, and they can flash. A small number of people can have a seizure when they see flashing lights or patterns, even with no history of epilepsy. The app limits how far and how fast its own effects change the brightness and follows Android's "Remove animations" setting, but it cannot make the visuals safe for everyone. If you or anyone watching is sensitive to flashing light, do not use the visuals, and stop at once if you feel unwell.
