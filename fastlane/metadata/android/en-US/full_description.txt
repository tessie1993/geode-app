BeatRealms 3D is a music player whose visuals move with the music you play, and a studio that turns those visuals into video. It works offline, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

Play your music
• Plays the music on your phone: tracks, albums, artists, folders and playlists, with search.
• Queue, shuffle, repeat, sleep timer, ReplayGain, an equalizer and fades between tracks.
• Lyrics from an .lrc file or the track's tags.
• A home screen widget and a Quick Settings tile.
• The visuals can also follow the microphone or the sound of another app, if you turn that on.

Watch it
• 334 visual styles: 3D scenes you fly through, fractals, nebulae, terrain, glass, fluid and particle simulations, and, with Pro, a MilkDrop-compatible engine for your own .milk preset files.
• The visuals listen to the sound itself: loudness, bass, mids and treble, drum hits, notes, chords and key, and the build-ups and drops of a song. No sound, no visuals: when the music stops, the picture comes to rest.
• Auto mode changes the style, the camera and the motion as the song moves from one part to the next. Your colours stay yours.
• Customize in up to eleven groups, from quick knobs to camera, motion, the endless dive, effects and a liquid mode you can push around with your fingers. With Pro you can build your own knobs.
• More than 700 bundled looks, and you can save, organise and share your own.

Make a video
• Render the visual over a song to an MP4 file on your phone.
• 720p, 1080p or 4K; 24 to 60 frames per second; H.264 or HEVC.
• Widescreen, vertical, square and other shapes.
• Loop renders and renders of a whole queue or playlist (Pro).
• Record (Pro): hold a finger on the visual to record a live video of what it shows and hears, until you stop.

Beyond the app (Pro)
• A live wallpaper and a screen saver that react to what is playing.
• Cast the visual and the music to a TV with Google Cast.
• Show the visual in a web browser on a computer on the same Wi-Fi, through a link or a QR code.
• Show the visual on a connected display: HDMI, USB-C or a wireless display.

Free and Pro
Without Pro, with no ads: the music player, more than 60 styles (including the first of each of the nineteen style families), the basic controls in Customize (quick knobs included), and video export up to 1080p at 30 frames per second, one minute long, with a watermark across the picture. Auto mode directs the first minute of each song.
Pro is a single in-app purchase, not a subscription: everything marked Pro above, every style and every control, 4K and 60 frames per second, full-length video without the watermark, and Auto mode for the whole song. You can try Pro for one hour before buying; videos rendered or recorded during the trial keep the one-minute limit and the watermark.

Privacy
No account, no ads, no analytics. Your music, your listening history, your settings and the videos you make stay on your phone (settings and history also in your Android backup, if you use it). The internet is used only for Cast to TV and for showing the visual on a computer: finding the TV and streaming to the TV or the browser over your own Wi-Fi. The Google Cast component sends Google anonymous usage logs; the privacy policy explains this.

Photosensitivity warning
The visuals are bright, fast and full-screen, and they can flash. A small number of people can have a seizure when they see flashing lights or patterns, even with no history of epilepsy. The app limits how far and how fast its own effects may change the brightness, and it follows Android's "Remove animations" setting, but it does not measure the finished picture, and MilkDrop presets only get a brightness cap: the flashes they draw themselves are not limited. It cannot make the visuals safe for everyone. If you or anyone watching is sensitive to flashing light, do not use the visuals, and stop at once if you feel unwell.

Requires Android 13 or newer.
